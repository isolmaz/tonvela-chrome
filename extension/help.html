TONVELA · 1.3 · Türkçe

Make the sound fit you.

Up to 400% boost, volume leveling and a speech filter that runs on your device.

Getting started

  1. Open a video and play it.
  2. Click the Tonvela icon in the Chrome toolbar. If you don't see it, pin Tonvela from the puzzle icon.
  3. Turn on the switch in the top right. The volume controls appear. Pick a volume; optionally turn on Keep volume steady.
  4. You can close the window; processing continues. Turning the switch off returns the tab to its normal audio and keeps your last preferences.

Tonvela starts separately on each tab. All audio on that tab is processed. Processing ends when the tab closes.

Advanced settings, appearance and language

While Tonvela is on, open Advanced settings at the bottom. Listening modes, a manual target level, site preferences, shortcuts and an audio reset live there. The section starts closed each time you reopen the window; your audio preferences are kept.

The default appearance is dark. Appearance → Dark / Light / System applies to the extension and this help page and is stored locally in the browser. System follows your computer's theme changes. Language → English / Türkçe switches the extension and this help page; English is the default.

Modes

Normal
Manual boost and a fast peak limiter.
Steady
Measures the sound and keeps it around the target by raising quiet parts and lowering loud ones. Boost is reduced during silent gaps.
Speech
A filter that brings out speech frequencies, plus automatic leveling. It does not separate background music.
Night
A lower target, stronger peak limiting and a slight bass cut.
Music
Light bass support; automatic leveling starts off so the music keeps its natural dynamics.
Voice only · Experimental
Tries to suppress everything except speech with the bundled GTCRN model. A real local neural network runs. It is not a source separation system that removes all music and effects perfectly. Music, singing and overlapping voices may remain, and some speech detail may be reduced. Output is mono in this mode and the model runs at 16 kHz. The model runs outside the audio thread and adds about 50 ms of delay. If your computer can't keep up, the window shows a warning; use Speech mode in that case.

“Keep this level”

Choose a volume that is comfortable. Listen to an audible part of the video for 1–2 seconds and press the button. The average of what you just heard becomes the target. If you don't like the result, the Undo button in the notice restores your previous settings. The target slider in Advanced settings lets you pick a calmer or louder level.

This is the video's digital level; it does not control Windows or headphone volume. The target is shown on a relative 0–100 scale, not measured room decibels. Complete silence is never turned into sound, and boost is limited to 400%, so very quiet sources may not reach the target.

Keep volume steady also works with the other modes. Changing mode applies that mode's starting target and leveling setting.

Local processing and permissions

Audio and the model are processed on your computer. The extension doesn't connect to a server, record audio, use the microphone or write measurements to disk. The model and all code ship inside the package; nothing is downloaded on first use. Only the site you're watching may need the internet.

  • activeTab / tabCapture: access the audio of the tab you choose, when you start Tonvela.
  • offscreen: keep processing after the small window closes.
  • storage: store preferences in this browser; no cloud sync. Site preferences keep only the domain name, never video addresses or history.

With “Remember settings for this site” on, up to 100 site preferences are kept. Turning it off deletes that site's saved preference and uses your last general settings. Chrome removes these records when the extension is uninstalled.

Shortcuts

Alt + Shift + V: open Tonvela.
Alt + Shift + ↑ / ↓: change volume by 10%.

You can change these, and assign a shortcut to the leveling switch, under Chrome's Extensions → Keyboard shortcuts. Shortcuts used by another app may not work.

Known limits

  • Requires desktop Chrome 116 or later. This is not an iOS / Android Chrome extension.
  • Chrome settings pages and some protected content can't be captured. Compatibility with every video site has not been verified.
  • Because audio capture is active, Chrome may show a sharing / capture indicator on the tab; this does not mean audio is sent to a server.
  • Boost is at most 4× digital gain, not exactly four times the perceived loudness. The limiter reduces gain when needed; it can't fix distortion already in the source.
  • Steady volume does not promise perfectly flat sound; short transitions and silences are kept. Voice only is an experimental speech enhancement.
  • If audio and video drift noticeably in Voice only mode, use Speech mode.
