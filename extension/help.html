Make the sound fit you.

Up to 4× volume, steady levels and clearer speech for the tab you're watching. Everything runs on your device.

Getting started

  1. Open a video and play it.
  2. Click the Tonvela icon in the Chrome toolbar. If you don't see it, pin Tonvela from the puzzle icon.
  3. Drag the volume bar or pick 100%, 200% or 400%. Tonvela starts as soon as the sound changes.
  4. You can close the window; processing continues. Back to original sound returns the tab to its own audio.

Tonvela works separately on each tab. At 100% in Normal mode, with no listening settings on, the tab plays its own audio and Tonvela doesn't capture it. Processing ends when the tab closes.

Listening modes

Tap Listening mode to choose how the sound is shaped.

Normal
Your volume and a fast peak limiter, nothing else.
Steady
Measures the sound and keeps it around a target level by raising quiet parts and lowering loud ones. Boost is reduced during silent gaps.
Speech
Brings out speech frequencies and keeps the level steady. It does not remove background music.
Night
A lower target, a lower peak ceiling and a slight bass cut.
Music
Light bass support with the music's natural dynamics.
Voice only · Experimental
Tries to suppress everything except speech with the bundled GTCRN model, running locally. It does not remove all music and effects. Singing and overlapping voices may remain and some speech detail may be lost. Output is mono, the model works at 16 kHz and adds about 50 ms of delay. If your computer can't keep up, Tonvela says so; use Speech mode in that case.

Target level and “Keep this level”

Steady, Speech, Night and Voice only keep the sound around a target level, which you can set under the mode list. To use what you hear right now, play an audible part for 1–2 seconds and press Keep this level. Undo restores the previous settings.

This is the tab's digital level; it doesn't change Windows or headphone volume. Silence is never turned into sound, and boost is limited to 4×, so very quiet sources may not reach the target.

Settings

Output device
Plays the tabs Tonvela processes on a chosen speaker or headset. Chrome only shows device names after you allow microphone access once; Tonvela opens a page that asks for it, closes the microphone at once and only reads the names. Without it, Tonvela uses the default device.
Mono sound
Mixes left and right so each ear hears everything, useful with one earbud.
Left / right balance
Turns one side down. It never raises a side above its normal level.
Hearing protection
Sets the peak ceiling: Off (just below full scale), Light (−3 dB), Medium (−6 dB) or Strict (−12 dB). Peaks stay under the ceiling at any volume. It limits peaks, not average loudness, so keep your device volume comfortable too.
Remember settings for each site
Keeps the volume, mode and target per site (up to 100 sites, by domain name only). Opening Tonvela on that site applies them again. Turning it off deletes the saved sites.
Appearance and language
System, light or dark. English, Türkçe, Español, Português or العربية; until you choose, Tonvela follows Chrome's language.

Output device, mono, balance and hearing protection apply to every tab Tonvela processes.

Privacy and permissions

Audio is processed on your computer. Tonvela doesn't connect to a server, record audio or write measurements to disk. The model and all code ship inside the extension; nothing is downloaded.

  • activeTab / tabCapture: access the audio of the tab you open Tonvela on.
  • offscreen: keep processing after the window closes.
  • storage: keep your preferences in this browser, without cloud sync. Site preferences store the domain name only, never page addresses or history.
  • Microphone (optional): only if you ask to see output device names. The microphone is closed right away and never listened to.

Shortcuts

Alt + Shift + V: open Tonvela.
Alt + Shift + ↑ / ↓: change volume by 10%.

Change them, or add one that switches between Normal and Steady, in Chrome's Extensions → Keyboard shortcuts. Shortcuts used by another app may not work.

Known limits

  • Requires desktop Chrome 116 or later.
  • Chrome pages and some protected content can't be captured. Not every video site has been tested.
  • While Tonvela processes a tab, Chrome may show a capture indicator on it. Audio is not sent anywhere.
  • 400% is 4× digital gain, not four times the perceived loudness. The limiter can't fix distortion already in the source.
  • Steady doesn't promise perfectly flat sound. Voice only is experimental.
  • If audio and video drift in Voice only mode, use Speech mode.
